# 8. Who could pay: business targets

Secondary research, 11 November 2026. A first pass, not a decision.

Written when Phew was a student-first product. Since 13 November 2026 Phew is for anyone who works on a Mac. On 14 November you removed academic buyers: universities and schools buying for their students are no longer targets. Which target to explore first is decided after the MVP.

## 8.1 The point

The pattern that works is an organisation that buys and an individual who uses. Explain and guide, in any app, with memory of the person's projects, is the same product in each case below. Only the content of the Map changes.

## 8.2 Targets

| # | Who buys | Who uses | Why they would pay | Evidence | Fit now |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Small Mac-first companies: studios, agencies, startups | Juniors, interns, new hires | Seniors lose hours showing tools and processes | Sits inside the digital adoption market below | Best |
| 2 | Larger companies, training and IT teams | Employees learning business software | Faster adoption of software they already bought | Market about $1.59B in 2026, about $4.37B by 2034, 13.5% a year | Weak for now |
| 3 | Software vendors | Their customers | Fewer support tickets, faster onboarding | Existing category, mostly web based | Poor |

## 8.3 What the evidence says

**Companies pay for on-screen guidance.** Digital adoption platforms, such as WalkMe and Whatfix, overlay step-by-step guidance on business software. The market is put at $1.24B in 2025 and $1.59B in 2026. Their guidance is authored by hand by the customer's team, and the leaders are now adding AI to write it. Phew's approach needs no authoring at all. That is the opening.

## 8.4 Why target 2 is weak for now

- Phew is Mac only. Large companies cannot buy a tool that part of their people cannot run.
- They need security reviews, contracts and long buying cycles.
- Reading employees' screens raises works council and privacy questions in Europe.

## 8.5 Recommendation

Target 1, small Mac-first companies, fits best: Mac-heavy and fast to decide. Whether to explore it, and when, is your decision after the MVP.

What changes in the product for a business version: the Map gets shared items written by the organisation, such as "how we set up a file" or "our export settings", next to the personal ones. Nothing in the MVP blocks this.
