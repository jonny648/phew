# Dia: what Phew can do

Written 15 November 2026. **Not tested.** A test here would show your open tabs or files to the model, so none was run (your choice of 15 November: you try it yourself).

## How he acts here

By pressing, typing and picking menus, and by clicking at a point where a control has no name. Web pages have named controls, so he presses and types by name there.

The clicking itself was measured on a made-up canvas: 5 of 5 on target, within 5 points. That canvas is a native Mac view. This app draws its own; whether the click lands the same way is not known.
