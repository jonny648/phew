# Excel: what Phew can do

Written 15 November 2026, phase PH (PH.6). Tested live with made-up items titled "Phew test" and a number; every item was removed afterwards by its id. How he acts here: through the app's own scripting, behind the app, without taking your screen or mouse.

**Result: 5 of 7 (the first two failed on a fault in the check after writing; fixed, then 5 of 5) made-up jobs done right.**

## He can

- Make a new workbook.
- Fill a column, a row, a single cell, a grid.
- Fill a small table with a SUM formula.
- Requests in Italian.

## He cannot, yet

- Work in a workbook that is already open: he always makes a new one.
- Save the workbook: it stays open and unsaved.
- Formatting, charts, several sheets.
- Read your sheets.

## To know

- macOS asks you once whether Phew may control or reach this app. Without your yes he stops and says so.
- After each action he reads back what he wrote, to check it.
