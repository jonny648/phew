# Notes: what Phew can do

Written 15 November 2026, phase PH (PH.6). Tested live with made-up items titled "Phew test" and a number; every item was removed afterwards by its id. How he acts here: through the app's own scripting, behind the app, without taking your screen or mouse.

**Result: 5 of 5 made-up jobs done right.**

## He can

- Make a new note with a title and text, several lines.
- Make a note and then add to it, in one job.
- Requests in Italian.
- Text with special signs (& " < >) kept as written.
- Two notes in one job.

## He cannot, yet

- Add to a note he did not make in this job. He stops instead.
- Edit, move or delete an existing note.
- Formatting (bold, lists, checklists) through scripting. By clicking in the Notes window he made text bold and italic once in a test; not measured further.
- Read your notes. A test that tried to look at a note in its own window was refused by the test guard, because Notes kept its main window, with your list of notes, in front.

## To know

- macOS asks you once whether Phew may control or reach this app. Without your yes he stops and says so.
- After each action he reads back what he wrote, to check it.
