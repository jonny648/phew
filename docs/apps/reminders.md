# Reminders: what Phew can do

Written 15 November 2026, phase PH (PH.6). Tested live with made-up items titled "Phew test" and a number; every item was removed afterwards by its id. How he acts here: through the app's own scripting, behind the app, without taking your screen or mouse.

**Result: 5 of 5 (3 of 5 on the first pass: two requests were taken as "remember this" and went to the Map; fixed, then 5 of 5) made-up jobs done right.**

## He can

- Add a reminder with a day and a time ("tomorrow at 10").
- Add a reminder with a day and no time.
- Requests in Italian.
- Add a reminder with no date.
- Two reminders in one job.

## He cannot, yet

- Choose the list: it goes to your default list.
- Change, complete or delete an existing reminder.
- Repeating reminders, priorities, places.

## To know

- macOS asks you once whether Phew may control or reach this app. Without your yes he stops and says so.
- After each action he reads back what he wrote, to check it.
