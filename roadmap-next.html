Phew! after the MVP

Written 16 November 2026, the day version 0.1.0 went online. General phases only: each gets its task list when it starts, in this same file. The MVP's own checklist stays in roadmap.html as its record. The order is a proposal. N1 is first because it decides what N2 and N3 must fix; N4 comes before N5 because ten people on one key multiply the cost.

N1 Real use

Order 1.

  • You use Phew for two weeks on your real work and write down each failure as it happens: what you asked, what he did, what you expected.
  • A list of failures sorted by how often they happen and how much they hurt. It decides the content of N2 and N3.
  • PM in the MVP roadmap, done for real.
  • You. No code, except fixes for what blocks daily use.

N2 Acting and agents, second pass

Order 2. Started 16 November 2026, while N1 (your real use, your own notes) runs beside it. Not in this phase: an agent that edits your existing files (L5), which needs your decision first.

  • The weak points the MVP left: acting in Dia, Figma, Fusion and other apps that draw their own interface; two displays; changing, sending and deleting, with a confirm for an agent; returning to the exact window; reading an app's menus before acting; research that uses several searches and reads pages; a wider trick test on real apps and pages.
  • Of the jobs you really ask for in N1, 4 in 5 are done right. The trick test is repeated on real pages and written down.
  • L18, L5 (editing files), L14 (reports that teach the memory).
  • senses, brain, shell, checker.
